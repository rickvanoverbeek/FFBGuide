A wheel oscillates when the force reacting to a movement arrives late enough to reinforce the next movement instead of stopping it. Low friction, low damping and a light rim all make that easier, and a high overall gain makes each swing bigger.

Work through these in order and stop at the first value that settles it. Every one of them costs some detail, and stacking all five gives you a wheel that is stable and tells you nothing.
