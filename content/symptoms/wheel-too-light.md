A light wheel is usually two separate problems wearing one coat: not enough overall torque, and nothing mechanical underneath it. The first makes peaks small; the second makes the wheel feel like it is spinning in air between them.

Raise the master scale until the hardest corner is as heavy as you want to hold, then add friction or inertia for the mechanical weight. Doing it the other way round tends to end with a wheel that is both heavy and numb.
