Around straight ahead the car is barely loaded, so the forces the game sends are genuinely small. Every source of resistance in the base — friction, damping, extra centre damping — subtracts from those small forces before they reach you.

There is an unavoidable tension here: the same settings that calm oscillation around centre are the ones that make centre feel dead. If you arrived at these values while fixing a shaking wheel, expect to trade one problem back for a little of the other.
