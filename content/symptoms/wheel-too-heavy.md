Strength and fatigue are not the same axis. A wheel can be tiring at a modest peak torque because friction, damping and inertia all add effort that carries no information, while the peak itself only arrives occasionally.

So lower the effects that are constant before lowering the master scale, and you keep the moments that matter while getting your arms back.
