---
label: "Damping"
category: damping
summary: "Resistance the wheel base generates itself, proportional to how fast the wheel is turning."
order: 10
---
Damping opposes angular velocity: faster movement meets more opposing torque, while a stationary wheel feels nothing. It is generated by the base regardless of what the title asks for, which is what separates it from a setting that scales the game's damper effect.

It is the standard remedy for oscillation, because oscillation is a velocity-driven phenomenon.
