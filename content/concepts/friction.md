---
label: "Friction"
category: friction
summary: "Constant resistance to movement, generated by the base and independent of wheel speed."
order: 10
---
Friction adds a roughly constant opposing torque from the moment the wheel moves. It raises the effort needed to break the wheel loose and dulls small, fast oscillations.

Friction and damping stack, so raising both together can make the wheel feel numb well before either value looks high on its own.
