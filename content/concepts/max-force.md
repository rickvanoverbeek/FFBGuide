---
label: "Maximum force output"
category: force_limit
summary: "The ceiling on torque the base will produce — the first setting to get right."
order: 10
---
Everything else is shaped inside this limit. Set it too high and peaks reach the base's maximum and flatten, losing the detail inside them; set it too low and small forces disappear beneath what you can feel.

Because the game applies its own scale as well, effective force is the product of both, which is where most clipping comes from.
