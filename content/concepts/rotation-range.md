---
label: "Rotation range"
category: other
summary: "How many degrees the wheel turns lock to lock, and how the game's steering lock maps onto it."
order: 10
---
Rotation range sets the physical travel between the two steering locks. When the title's own lock and the base's range disagree you get either a soft stop before the mechanical limit or a mismatch between your hands and the car's front wheels.

It is not a force setting, but it changes perceived weight: the same torque spread over a wider range feels slower and lighter at the rim.
