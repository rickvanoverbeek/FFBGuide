---
label: "Slew rate limit"
category: slew_rate
summary: "Caps how fast torque may change, blunting sudden spikes without lowering peak force."
order: 10
---
The limit constrains the rate of change of torque, not its magnitude: full force is still reachable, but needs a minimum amount of time to arrive.

That makes it a comfort and safety control. Peak cornering load is unaffected while crashes and kerb strikes lose their edge — and abrupt detail arrives slightly late.
