---
label: "Force linearity"
category: force_limit
summary: "Changes the mapping between requested and delivered torque instead of its maximum."
order: 20
---
A linear mapping delivers exactly what the title asks. Bending the curve either amplifies weak forces relative to strong ones or compresses the peaks, which changes how much of the range you spend in the informative middle.

It is easy to confuse with a strength setting, but it changes proportions rather than the ceiling.
