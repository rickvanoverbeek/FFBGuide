---
label: "Spring effect scaling"
category: spring_centering
summary: "Scales the spring effect a game sends rather than generating centering in the base."
order: 20
---
A spring effect grows with displacement from a centre point, and via DirectInput a title can place that centre somewhere other than the wheel's centre.

Most sims model self-centering in physics instead of requesting a spring effect, which is why this setting often appears to do nothing in them.
