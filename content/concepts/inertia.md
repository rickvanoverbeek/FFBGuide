---
label: "Inertia"
category: inertia
summary: "Simulated extra rotating mass — resistance to changes in wheel speed."
order: 10
---
Inertia opposes angular acceleration rather than velocity or position, so it shows up at the moment you start or stop a steering input and leaves steady cornering load alone.

It is commonly used to make a light rim feel closer to a real steering system that carries mass of its own.
