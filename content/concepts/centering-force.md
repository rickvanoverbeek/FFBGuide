---
label: "Centering force"
category: spring_centering
summary: "A centering pull the base generates itself, independent of the game's forces."
order: 10
---
This is a displacement-based force: the further from centre, the stronger the pull back. Because the base generates it, it works even when the title sends nothing.

Racing titles normally derive self-centering from tyre and suspension forces, so this is mostly for titles and desktop use that do not.
