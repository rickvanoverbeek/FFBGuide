VNM describes Sine Gain as smooth sine wave effects for testing or vibration feedback.

Three more waveform controls sit alongside it with the same role: Square Gain and Triangle Gain for waveform vibrations or test signals, and Sawtooth Up Gain and Sawtooth Down Gain for sharp rising or falling waveforms used by some games.
