NDP is generated by the wheel base rather than requested by the game, and reacts to the acceleration and angle of your steering.

Because it does not depend on the title sending a damper effect, it applies consistently everywhere — the practical difference from DPR, and the reason it is the parameter to reach for against oscillation on a direct drive base.
