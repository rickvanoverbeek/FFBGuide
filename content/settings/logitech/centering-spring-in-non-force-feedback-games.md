The setting only applies where a title sends no force feedback. Logitech is explicit that once a force feedback game loads it takes full control of the wheel and this setting has no effect, leaving in-game settings as the only adjustment.

The spring is also disabled when you tab away from or close such a game, and comes back when you return to it or move a slider in G HUB.
