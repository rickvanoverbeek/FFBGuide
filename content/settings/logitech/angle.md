Angle sets the overall rotation response of the wheel, determining how much steering input a given in-game turn requires.

Logitech notes that most titles handle matching the car's own range, so leaving it wide and letting the title manage it is the usual approach; a Formula car using 360 degrees is the case where a fixed narrower value helps. On G923-era wheels the same control appears as the sensitivity slider on the wheel's G HUB page.
