The control panel exposes one percentage per effect family. Thrustmaster's own documentation divides them into static effects, Constant and Periodic, which the game sets and which ignore what the wheel is doing, and dynamic effects, Spring and Damper, which respond to wheel position and speed.

Every one of them scales something the title requests. Nothing here generates friction or inertia in the wheel, which is why those rows are empty for Thrustmaster.
