SimPro Manager is the successor to Alpha Manager and covers the whole Simagic ecosystem, with force feedback profiles saved locally or to the cloud.

The distinction to carry across is Mechanical Damper versus Game Damper: the first is generated by the base, the second scales what the title sends. Simagic's published configurations lean on moderate mechanical damping and low game damping. Detailed reference documentation is thin — several settings are explained only through their own setup articles.
