Pit House separates Basic settings from a Professional view that exposes more range and more controls. The naming is consistent about origin: everything prefixed Mechanical is generated by the base, while Game force settings act on what the simulator sends.

Several controls are safety rather than feel: a maximum output torque limit, a wheel speed limit, hands-off protection and auxiliary centering control. Labels can differ slightly between Pit House versions.
