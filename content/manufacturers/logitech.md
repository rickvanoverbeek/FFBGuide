G HUB is deliberately sparse, and its vocabulary changed with the Pro wheels: Angle, Force Feedback Filter, Dampener and TF Audio. On G923-era wheels the rotation range is the sensitivity slider instead.

The centering spring is the clearest example of Logitech's approach — it is labelled for non force feedback games and is explicitly inert once a force feedback title takes control of the wheel. There are no base-generated friction or inertia effects, so those rows are legitimately empty.
