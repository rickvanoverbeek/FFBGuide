Fanatec's abbreviations fall into two groups, and telling them apart resolves most confusion. FOR, SPR and DPR scale effects the game sends, so they do nothing in titles that do not request them. NDP, NFR and NIN are generated by the wheel base itself and therefore apply everywhere.

The same parameters appear in the wheel base Tuning Menu and in the Control Panel on the PC. Parameters that are not force shaping — SHO, BLI, BRF and MPS — are not documented here.
