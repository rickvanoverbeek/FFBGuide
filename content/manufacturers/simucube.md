Simucube Tuner groups its settings into Basic, Mechanical Feel, Smoothness, Advanced and Game Effects. That split matters when translating: the Mechanical Feel damping and friction sliders are generated by the base and apply in every title, while the identically named Game Effects sliders only scale what a simulator requests.

The filtering section is unusually deep — reconstruction, torque bandwidth, slew rate and a notch filter are separate controls rather than one smoothing slider. Some settings, including Center Damping and Static Force Reduction Speed, are Ultimate-only.
